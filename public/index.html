How it works

Each Jev turn calls experimental_evaluate({model:'typesafe-ai/jev'}) with the table as structured state and 4 parallel questions (choice + booleans + score). Low-confidence raises are gated to pot-control — watch the confidence bar dip before it checks behind.

Table talk is snapshotted into the per-street history on every action, so Jev reads your story as a sequence (flop: you say "..." → turn: you say "...") and its bluff-detector weighs contradictions across streets.
